# 🌌 LONE ORBIT — The Forgotten Explorers

### One mind. Infinite possibilities.

**The Forgotten Explorers** is an interactive digital space museum dedicated to preserving the stories of humanity's space exploration hardware.

Across the Moon, Mars and deep space, machines built by humans have explored distant worlds, gathered scientific knowledge and expanded our understanding of the universe. Some remain where their missions ended. Others continue travelling beyond Earth.

Their missions may have ended. Their stories have not.

🔗 **Live Website:** https://suchitra2207.github.io/the-forgotten-explorers/

---

## 🚀 The Project

The project explores the idea that space exploration is not just about astronauts, planets and successful missions. It is also about the equipment, machines and technological achievements that made those discoveries possible.

Through an interactive museum experience, visitors can discover the stories behind these explorers and understand their contributions to space science.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Preserve the stories of space exploration hardware.
- Make space science accessible and engaging for different age groups.
- Highlight humanity's technological achievements beyond Earth.
- Distinguish between equipment left behind, completed missions and spacecraft still travelling.
- Encourage curiosity about the history and future of space exploration.

## 🪐 Explore the Collection

The museum features nine exhibits across three destinations.

### 1. The Moon 🌕

Explore equipment associated with humanity's lunar exploration.

- Apollo Lunar Module (Eagle)
- Lunar Roving Vehicle
- Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package (ALSEP)

### 2. Mars 🔴

Discover robotic explorers that investigated the Red Planet.

- Sojourner
- Spirit
- Opportunity

### 3. Deep Space ✨

Follow spacecraft that expanded humanity's reach beyond the planets.

- Voyager 1
- Voyager 2
- New Horizons

## ✨ Key Features

- Interactive exhibit cards and detailed pop-ups.
- Search functionality for discovering space missions.
- Interactive mission timeline with destination filters.
- Dedicated Moon, Mars and Deep Space collections.
- NASA source references and image credits.
- Responsive interface for desktop and mobile.
- Welcome experience introducing the digital museum.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

LONE ORBIT uses a minimalist cosmic visual identity inspired by the mystery and scale of space.

The visual language combines:
- Midnight purple and deep violet.
- Muted lavender highlights.
- Warm ivory typography.
- Space-inspired imagery.
- Interactive museum-style navigation.
